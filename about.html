About Our Company

Our Story

PowerPay Africa is an African-based energy and IoT technology company that provides hardware and software to finance and monitor electric appliances/equipment. The company, a spin off of Green Innovation Ventures Enterprises Ltd established in 2018 initially as an energy access company, works with manufacturers, distributors and lenders to make cooking and productive-use equipment monitorable, accessible and affordable.
